Role Overview

We’re hiring an Enterprise Account Executive and B2B Sales Leader to create real business value for Mercor. This is a quota-carrying IC role owning the full sales cycle — prospecting, executive engagement, technical scoping, negotiation, and close.

Note: This opportunity requires an estimated 40 hours of work every week.

What You’ll Do

  • Generate and qualify pipeline through outbound, executive relationships, events, and partnerships.
  • Run consultative cycles with senior operators and technical leaders; build the business case that gets budget approved.
  • Partner with Engineers to scope deployments, run POCs with clear success metrics, and negotiate multi-year agreements to signature.
  • Expand accounts beyond the first deal, keep clean CRM hygiene, and forecast accurately.
  • Feed market signal back to product and GTM leadership.

What We’re Looking For

  • 3+ years of enterprise B2B sales experience, ideally selling technical or platform products to the Fortune 500.
  • Track record of consistently hitting quota and closing complex, multi-stakeholder deals ($500K+ ACV).
  • Comfort selling to both C-suite and technical buyers; able to translate ambiguous business problems into a concrete value thesis.
  • Strong discovery, commercial negotiation, and pipeline-generation skills.
  • Thrives in a fast-moving, high-autonomy startup environment.

Contract and Payment Terms

  • You will be engaged as an independent contractor.
  • This is a fully remote role that can be completed on your own schedule.
  • Projects can be extended, shortened, or concluded early depending on needs and performance.
  • Payments are weekly on Stripe or Wise based on services rendered.
